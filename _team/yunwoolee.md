Yunwoo Lee is an Assistant Professor in EECS at DGIST. He received his Ph.D. and B.S. degrees in Mechanical&Aerospace Engineering and Electrical&Computer Engineering, respectively, from Seoul National University (SNU). Before joining DGIST, he was a postdoc at the Robotics Institute at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU RI). His research focuses on robotic applications in challenging environments.
