Yunwoo is a PI at Open Autonomy Lab. He earned his PhD (Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering) and bachelor's degree (Electrical and Computer Engineering) from Seoul National University, South Korea. His research interests include multi-robot systems, aerial tracking, and mobile robot planning. He is passionate about developing fully autonomous robots.
